Setup guide

One approval, then never again.

Android 15 and newer hide SMS access from apps installed outside the Play Store. This is a one-time unlock, and it takes about thirty seconds. Here is exactly what to do.

  1. 01

    Open the app's info page.

    Long-press the MyMoney icon on your home screen, then tap App info (the small ⓘ symbol). You can also reach it from Settings, then Apps, then MyMoney.

  2. 02

    Allow restricted settings.

    Inside the info page, tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner, then choose Allow restricted settings. Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint when asked.

  3. 03

    Grant SMS permission.

    Go back to the info page, open Permissions, then find SMS. The toggle will finally be editable. Turn it on.

  4. 04

    Open MyMoney and scan.

    Tap Scan SMS once. Your MoMo and Telecel receipts appear instantly. Nothing leaves your device.

A note for Samsung and Xiaomi users. The ⋮ menu on Samsung phones may appear as "More." On Xiaomi and Redmi devices, "Allow restricted settings" often sits at the very bottom of the info page, so scroll to find it. If the option is missing entirely, open the app once, close it, then try again.
